E-Commerce Customer Analytics

96.9% of 99,441 Olist customers bought only once, so I built an RFM segmentation with an action plan per segment.

Business value: shows which customers to win back (a second-order offer was projected to lift revenue by about 25%) and flags orders at risk of arriving late.

PythonSQL ServerScikit-learnSeaborn
  • Late-delivery model: Gradient Boosting, AUC 0.768.
  • Product-price model: Random Forest, R² 0.744.
  • KPIs: ARPU R$166.59, 67.9% average product margin.
